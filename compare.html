Resource constraints

Why DP runs out of memory: the table has about n · 2n cells. For n = 20 that is more than 20 million cells, and every extra city doubles the memory.

Why B&B is unpredictable: good pruning cuts almost all branches, but a bad input may force it to explore close to n! nodes, and the live-node queue also uses memory.

When to use which: DP for small and medium n when we need a guaranteed running time; B&B when pruning works well on the data, because it can solve bigger cases.
