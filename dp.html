Explanation

Idea: C(S, j) = the shortest path that starts at city 1, visits every city of the subset S exactly once and ends at city j.

Formula: C(S, j) = min { C(S - {j}, i) + d(i, j) } for every i in S, i not j. We try every city i as the "previous" city and keep the smallest.

Rounds: we solve subsets of size s = 2, 3, ... n. Smaller subsets are solved first and their answers are reused. This reuse is called overlapping subproblems.

Final answer: min over j of C({1..n}, j) + d(j, 1). The tour is found by going backwards through the table.

Complexity: there are 2n-1 subsets, n cities, and n choices of i, so Time = O(n² · 2n) and Space = O(n · 2n) for the table.
